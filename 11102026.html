Executive summary

1

Four new KEV additions on 9 November, all due 12 November — and all already exploited in the wild. GPUStack CVE-2026-102895 (9.8, authentication bypass), SignalWire FreeSWITCH CVE-2026-116779 (9.8, code injection), IBM Aspera Faspex CVE-2026-123218 (9.8, authorisation bypass) and OpenSSH CVE-2026-123158 (8.1, SSHole) were added to CISA’s Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue on 9 November, with an unusually short three-day federal deadline. SSHole has the richest new evidence: GreyNoise recorded 16 successful exploitation chains from 6 IPs, one installing a Python C2 implant (“TAC Agent”), and Previdian counted 20 attempts from 13 IPs across 9 countries. Fortinet’s PSIRT advisories for the AI-found FortiOS / FortiManager / FortiAnalyzer flaws had still not been published as of 09:02 SGT — no CVE IDs yet.

2

Enforcement and a regulator breach lead the incident picture. FinCEN designated crypto exchange MEXC Global a primary money-laundering concern over at least $16bn in alleged illicit transactions (May 2021–May 2026), including Russian, Iranian and North Korean sanctions evasion; US financial institutions will be barred from transacting with it once the order takes effect, expected in December — this is not an OFAC asset freeze. The Philippine SEC confirmed a breach of its platform users, exposing names, contact details and tax identification numbers; no user count was given, the platform was not named, and the 487,000-line forum claim is unconfirmed. Wiz found Qubole-associated AWS credentials abused across customer accounts in October — a cloud supply-chain exposure for any firm that ever connected Qubole to AWS, including through dormant roles.

3

Follow-ups dominate the rest: partial recovery at Arkéa, an FSS inspection at Mirae Asset, and a denied Binance claim. Arkéa has recovered part of the stolen funds (amount unstated); note the Reuters piece confirming about €100m is dated 5 November, not 9 November — 9 November hits are re-syndications. The FSS began an on-site inspection at Mirae Asset Securities on 9 November, probing whether four Mirae Asset group incidents are linked (about 39,000 people affected in total). Mercado Bitcoin’s view-only access started on 9 November; Binance denied a ShinyHunters claim of 335.9 million records, calling it likely a scam, with no evidence published. N0n listed unnamed Canadian wealth-management firms and a US payment-card network — all unconfirmed, no victim named. No new AI-scaled phishing, vishing or deepfake campaign was found in-window.

CVE-2026-116779 NEW — KEV 9 NOV9.8SignalWire FreeSWITCH before 1.11.3In the Wild (KEV)New in-window (added to CISA KEV 9 Nov; federal due date 12 Nov; ransomware use Unknown). Inbound SIP MESSAGE handling splits the To-user on + and treats the left side as a chat-protocol selector with no allow-list; selecting the api chat protocol reaches switch_api_execute(), so a peer whose MESSAGE is accepted can run FreeSWITCH API commands including OS commands (system) with daemon privileges. Finance relevance is moderate: FreeSWITCH underpins call centres, hosted PBX, carrier SIP gateways and conferencing; banks’ contact-centre / telephony estates are the exposure.

MEXC cut off from the US financial system — NEW (FinCEN order, 6 Nov; reported 9 Nov)

On 6 November FinCEN issued an order under §9714(a) of the Combating Russian Money Laundering Act designating Seychelles-incorporated MEXC Global a “primary money laundering concern in connection with Russian illicit finance”, as part of Treasury’s “Operation Economic Outcast”.
